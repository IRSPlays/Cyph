Limiting reagent
from Chem_WS_7.pdf · read 2 min
The limiting reagent is the reactant that is used up first. It decides how much product can form.

To find it: convert every reactant to moles, divide by its number in the balanced equation, and the smallest result is the limiting one.

Everything after that step uses the moles of the limiting reagent, never the one in excess.
