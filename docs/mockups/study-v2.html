CYPH · STUDY MODE · PROPOSAL

Open Study, press one button, get better at what you are worst at.

Today Study is seven tabs of tools and you have to work out which one to use. This plan turns it into one thing: a short session that Cyph has already built for you. Everything else exists to feed that session.

Based on your answers: Cyph tells you what to do · PC and iPad as separate full apps · 10 to 20 minute bursts · plan from due cards and weak topics · one button to start · one inbox for material · explain what you learned out loud after school.

What is wrong now

  • No starting point. TODAY, NOTES, TUTOR, CARDS, PRACTICE, NOTEBOOKS and BOARDS all sit at the same level. Nothing says which one matters right now.
  • The tools do not talk to each other. A wrong answer in PRACTICE does not change what CARDS shows you. Weak topics are a list you can look at, not something that drives the work.
  • Adding material is a chore. You pick the subject, wait, then go to a different tab to approve draft cards.
  • The phone is a shrunken desktop. It has the same tabs instead of the two things a phone is good for: a quick session and a camera.

The new shape

Three things you do, and one place where everything is kept.

1 · EVERY DAY

Session

One START button. About 15 minutes: the cards that are due, then questions on your weakest topic, then one question you got wrong before. Cyph picks all of it.

2 · AFTER SCHOOL

Debrief

Hold the mic and explain what you learned today. Cyph works out the topics, asks you about the parts you skipped or got wrong, and marks those as weak.

3 · WHENEVER

Inbox

Drop a PDF, snap a worksheet, forward a past paper. Cyph guesses the subject and topic and prepares cards and questions. You confirm with one tap.

THE SHELF

Library

Each subject is a list of topics with a strength bar. Notes, cards, papers, notebooks and boards live under their topic. You come here to look something up, not to decide what to do.

Debrief and Inbox tell Cyph what you know and what you have. The Session spends that. A STUCK? key opens the tutor from any screen.

Phone

Three jobs: a quick session, the camera, and the mic

The phone drops the tabs. It opens on START. A phone session is cards and short questions only, so it works on a bus. The camera sends straight to the Inbox. Library is there for looking things up, with no editing.

How Cyph decides

Every topic gets a strength from 0 to 100. It is the one number the whole mode runs on.

  • It goes up when you grade a card GOOD, answer a question correctly without hints, or explain the topic well in a debrief.
  • It goes down when you grade AGAIN, need hints, get a question wrong, or miss it in a debrief. It also fades slowly if you have not touched the topic.

A session is built in this order until the time is used up (15 minutes on PC and iPad, 8 on phone):

  • Cards due, weakest topics first, capped at about a third of the time so cards never crowd out real questions.
  • One weak spot: the lowest-strength topic that has material. One to three questions taken from your own worksheets and past papers, with the hint ladder.
  • One second try: a question you got wrong a few days ago, asked again.

If nothing is due it goes straight to the weak spot. If a weak topic has no material, Cyph asks for a debrief or a worksheet instead of inventing content.

Build order

  • 1. The engine. Topic strength, the session builder, answer checking. Server only, with tests.
  • 2. Now and the session runner on desktop, then phone.
  • 3. Inbox with auto-filing and one-tap confirm, plus phone camera capture.
  • 4. Debrief with voice, follow-up questions and gap detection.
  • 5. Library regrouped by topic. Old tabs removed.
  • 6. iPad handwritten answers inside the session.

Existing data (cards, notes, sources, notebooks, boards) is kept and moved under topics. Nothing you have made is deleted.
