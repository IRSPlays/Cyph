CYPH · PHONE · NOTIFICATIONSHe taps you on the shoulder, he doesn't shout.

Every notification is a small card in the same dark panel as the app: a coloured edge for what it is, his face as the avatar, serif for what he says, mono for the facts, and chips for the one or two replies that matter. He only speaks on a schedule or when something he did finishes, never at random. Mood on the avatar is the mood he would have in the app at that moment.

Each kind is its own Android notification channel, so you can silence one (say Study) without touching the others, in the system settings. The Settings page in the app mirrors the same six switches.

RULES

HE SPEAKS ON SCHEDULENudges and reminders come from the plan, not from a timer in the phone. At most one nudge per block, and none while you are in Study or Do Not Disturb.
ONE FACE, NOT A MOOD PARADEThe avatar is a still of the mood he has then: happy after a finished block, smug for a nudge, sleepy for quiet. During an arm task it stays neutral and the card shows the camera, since moods are off while the arm works.
ANSWERS WITHOUT OPENINGChips send one command to the server and then dismiss the card: snooze, done, stop, start. Nothing needs a keyboard in a notification.
PRIVATE ON THE LOCK SCREENKinds and times only until unlocked. Telegram content and chat text never appear in a notification, only plan titles and task names.
HONEST ABOUT LIMITSThe face is a PNG, not the live canvas. Custom card layouts are limited in height when collapsed, so the long forms (brief, task) only appear expanded. Colours of the edge come from the channel, which is why there are exactly six.
ONE TAP TARGETTapping the card body opens the matching page (Plan, Study, Arm). Nothing else on the card does more than one thing.
