Acids, bases and the pH scale

1 · WHAT IS pH?

pH measures the concentration of hydrogen ions in a solution.

pH = −log10[H+]

A pH below 7 is acidic, 7 is neutral, above 7 is alkaline. Each step is a tenfold change p.2

2 · NEUTRALISATION

HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H2O

To find the moles of a solution, use n = c × V1000 with V in cm3 p.5

3 · WORKED EXAMPLE

25.0 cm3 of 0.10 mol/dm3 NaOH is neutralised by HCl. Moles of NaOH = 0.10 × 251000 = 0.0025 mol p.7
